02 — Featured Project

Medical Information Simplifier

A Prompt Engineering / GenAI project designed to make complex medical articles easier for ordinary readers to understand while preserving the source material’s meaning, uncertainty and limitations.

Prompt Engineering • GenAI
01 · InputProvided medical article + its included sources
02 · AnalyzeIdentify terminology, claims, gaps and conflicts
03 · SimplifyRewrite difficult language without changing meaning
04 · VerifyCheck against the original before output

The problem

Medical articles can be difficult for non-medical readers because of technical terminology, complex statements and information that may be easy to misunderstand.

The objective

Create a controlled AI workflow that improves readability without inventing facts or silently changing what the source says.

Key prompt-engineering decisions

  • Source-only rule: the model is instructed not to introduce outside information.
  • Meaning preservation: simplification must not change the original meaning.
  • Uncertainty preservation: words such as “may” or “could” are not converted into certainty.
  • Hallucination control: missing information is not filled with assumptions.
  • Medical safety: new diagnoses, medicines, dosages or treatment recommendations are not invented.
  • Conflict handling: conflicting statements are identified instead of being resolved by guessing.
  • Final verification: the generated output is checked against the original article.
This project demonstrates how I use prompt design not only to generate text, but to define boundaries, handle edge cases and evaluate AI output.
